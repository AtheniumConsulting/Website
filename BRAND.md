# Brand values for the site

Only the values the site must carry. The full identity lives in the athenium-design skill. Nothing here limits how bold the design may be.

## Accent

Gold `#FBA91A` is the single accent. Brand gradient runs from `#FBA91A` to `#EB5C46`.

## Neutrals

Dark `#2D363F`, Deep Black `#0F1419`, Mid Grey `#505B60`, Off White `#F9F9F9`, White `#FFFFFF`. Orange `#EF5828` is the hover companion.

## Type

Lato only. The font files are in the athenium-design skill.

## Logo

Use the white horizontal logo on dark surfaces and the black one on light. Never place it on gold. The logo files are in the athenium-design skill.

## Strapline

"Connected technologies meet industry expertise."
